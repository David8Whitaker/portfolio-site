Thaigether, the people you love, always close

A private live location + comms app built for couples and close circles — shared real-time GPS on a cinematic map, media-rich chat with 60-second voice notes, HD video & voice calls that erupt above the lock screen, deep-sleep push through Firebase, and a full weather vault (live rain radar + 7 days observed + 16 forecast) for wherever your person stands. One free stack, zero monthly cost.

◆ Why it exists

Two people, 780 km apart, whose calls kept dying in their phones' sleep. Existing apps treated "video call" as an afterthought behind lock screens and battery managers. Thaigether started as "what if being together was the entire app?"

◆ What I built

Presence + voice engineered like infrastructure: 1-second GPS with honest accuracy rings, data-only FCM push that wakes deep-sleep phones, WebRTC calling over STUN + open TURN relays, and a weather vault attached to live radar. Bilingual Thai ⇄ English on every surface.

◆ Where it stands

Version 17, live in daily use. Ships as an instant web app and a signed native Android shell from one Next.js 16 codebase — on a stack engineered to cost $0/month (Vercel, Firebase free tier, OpenStreetMap, RainViewer).

  • 🛰 Live presence that survives the night. Real-time dots + trails at 1s cadence, accuracy honesty rings, deep-sleep FCM push so messages and rings wake the phone even swiped-away.
  • 📞 Calls that behave like a carrier. Full-screen ring above the lock screen, decline without unlocking, dedicated audio-only mode, WebRTC over STUN + open TURN relays.
  • ⛈ Weather you can use, anywhere. Tap any point of the map: current sky, drill to any hour of 23 days, a rain radar timeline with a glowing NOW marker — freeze a frame by touch.
  • 💬 Chat that feels handmade. Photos, short clips, voice notes, custom-emoji reactions, edit & unsend, seen receipts, and a live "love ledger" (days · messages · minutes).
  • 🌐 Bilingual to the bone. Full Thai ⇄ English on every surface, tuned with a heart that lives between both phones.

Case study · operations suite · live pilot

TalatSuite, running real markets end to end

A complete platform for Thai day and night markets that I designed, built and tested end to end. Customers order from any shop. Vendors run a POS and kitchen queue. Managers run revenue and lot maps. Admins approve everything. And one codebase ships as a web app, an offline single file and a signed Android APK.

🏪 เจ้าของตลาด? อ่านรายละเอียดภาษาไทย →

◆ Why it exists

Real Thai markets run on paper slips, shouted orders and LINE groups. Vendor approvals happen by word of mouth; rent lives in a notebook. I owned businesses like this for 15 years — so I built the system I always wanted.

◆ What I built

One platform, four roles: guests order with just a phone number, vendors run a one-hand POS with a kitchen kanban, managers watch live revenue and approve lots on a real map, and admins hold an audit log of everything.

◆ Where it stands

Live pilot on Render, 52 API endpoints over 13 tables, 414 automated E2E checks guarding every build — shipped as web app, a self-contained offline demo, and a 457 KB signed APK. Thai-first, English included.

  • Guest checkout with nothing but a phone number
  • Real-time order timeline: received, cooking, ready
  • "Order again" one-tap cart refill

A POS that fits in one hand

A kitchen queue kanban where orders drag through cooking stages, a menu with photos and automatic stock (items sell out by themselves), tap-to-call customer numbers, and one-tap CSV and PDF sales reports.

  • Apply to more markets from inside the app, picking a free lot on the real map
  • Multi-market vendors switch shops with one button
  • Every application shows its status, or the real reason it was declined

Run the whole market from one dashboard

Live revenue and orders, shops-open-now metrics, a drag-and-drop lot map with per-lot rent, and an approval queue for every vendor application.

  • Approve or decline vendor applications with a reason
  • Day-of-week pricing and a schedule editor
  • Payments ledger and market profile with map pin

Total oversight, zero chaos

Every signup and application lands in one console. Approve new markets, manage all accounts, watch every action in a full audit log, and export anything to CSV.

  • Nothing publishes without approval, markets or shops
  • Role-scoped data: managers see only their own market
